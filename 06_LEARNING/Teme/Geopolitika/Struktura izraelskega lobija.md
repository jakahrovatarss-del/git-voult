# Struktura izraelskega lobija

- Izraelski lobi je opredeljen kot ohlapna koalicija posameznikov in skupin, ki si aktivno prizadevajo usmerjati zunanjo politiko ZDA v pro-izraelsko smer.[\[1\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=8&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A54.812999999999995%2C%22top%22%3A285.59999999999997%2C%22width%22%3A337.938%2C%22height%22%3A161.84000000000003%7D)[\[2\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=17&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A60.702000000000005%2C%22top%22%3A393.71999999999997%2C%22width%22%3A337.938%2C%22height%22%3A201.95999999999998%7D)

- Lobi ni enoten gibanje s centralnim vodstvom ali zarota, temveč močna interesna skupina, ki vključuje tako ameriške Jude kot krščanske sioniste.[\[2\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=17&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A60.702000000000005%2C%22top%22%3A393.71999999999997%2C%22width%22%3A337.938%2C%22height%22%3A201.95999999999998%7D)[\[3\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=27&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A72.933%2C%22top%22%3A596.36%2C%22width%22%3A323.895%2C%22height%22%3A13.600000000000023%7D)

- Organizacije, kot je AIPAC, imajo izjemen vpliv v kongresu, kjer lahko v zelo kratkem času zberejo podpise velike večine senatorjev za svoje pobude.[\[4\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=22&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A53.907%2C%22top%22%3A476.67999999999995%2C%22width%22%3A337.03200000000004%2C%22height%22%3A133.96000000000004%7D)[\[5\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=26&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A55.719%2C%22top%22%3A107.44%2C%22width%22%3A337.938%2C%22height%22%3A230.51999999999998%7D)

- Strategija lobiranja vključuje usmerjanje sredstev za politične kampanje, pritisk na izvršilno oblast in oblikovanje javnega diskurza prek medijev ter akademije.[\[6\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=28&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A57.984%2C%22top%22%3A326.4%2C%22width%22%3A337.93800000000005%2C%22height%22%3A188.36%7D)

- Člani lobija niso vedno reprezentativni za celotno judovsko skupnost v Ameriki, saj so številne organizacije sčasoma postale bolj desno usmerjene.[\[7\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=9&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A61.608000000000004%2C%22top%22%3A447.44%2C%22width%22%3A338.391%2C%22height%22%3A147.56%7D)[\[3\]](https://app.heptabase.com/7b0edf5d-3ea2-4bee-ae45-0d7fec330766/card/cec0db7a-73e4-4756-a4ab-35cba4f5047e?pdfPage=27&pdfBoundingBox=%7B%22left%22%3A72.933%2C%22top%22%3A596.36%2C%22width%22%3A323.895%2C%22height%22%3A13.600000000000023%7D)
